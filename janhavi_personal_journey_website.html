01 / My story

Every stage leaves something behind.

I began by learning about the world around me. Architecture taught me to observe space. Work taught me to take responsibility. My Master's is teaching me to ask who a city is really built for.

What I explore Affordable housing, urban systems, real estate, and people-centred cities.
How I work With curiosity, observation, research, design thinking, and a willingness to go to the roots of a question.
What I believe Planning should be for the people, by the people, and responsive to the realities of everyday life.

03 / Reflections

What the journey has taught me.

01

Stay curious

Good work begins with the willingness to ask questions and investigate what lies beneath the obvious.

02

Design is responsibility

Every design decision affects people, resources, access, and the way a place is experienced.

03

Learn from reality

Site visits, conversations, observations, and lived experiences often reveal what drawings and data alone cannot.

04

Keep adapting

New environments and responsibilities can challenge existing assumptions and create better ways of working.

05

People are central

The built environment is not only about form. It is also about livelihoods, relationships, access, and dignity.

06

Progress is ongoing

A journey does not need to be perfect to be meaningful. Reflection makes each stage part of the next one.
